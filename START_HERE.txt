FITTRACK PRO: START HERE

You need: a Windows laptop with Python 3.12 or 3.13
(download it from python.org and tick "Add python.exe to PATH" during the install).
Internet is needed only for step 2.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example the Desktop).
2. Double-click  setup.bat  and wait until it says "Setup finished" (about 2 minutes).
3. Double-click  start_demo.bat. A black window opens and shows two addresses.
   If Windows asks about the firewall, tick both boxes and click Allow.
4. On the laptop, open  http://127.0.0.1:8000  in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
5. On a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as the laptop,
   open the green address shown in the black window.
